Gaylord Ho

What is Parian II? Parian II was chosen for its classical styling and marble like finish. Parian II is a material that was created by Gaylord and the foundry through years of long hard work and experimentation. The original material, Parian ware, was created in 1851 as a method of casting sculpture to look like sculpted marble but at more affordable prices. The negative traits of the material were that it was rather chalky, fragile and easily damaged.
