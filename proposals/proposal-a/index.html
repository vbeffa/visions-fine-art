Visions Fine Art Proposals — Proposal A: Hosting Migration
Proposal A · hosting migration

Move the existing Visions website to modern hosting.

The goal is to move the current website to a reliable modern hosting environment while preserving the existing site and minimizing disruption. Because the exact configuration of the current hosting, DNS, email, and any legacy server-side functionality cannot be fully known until access is available, this work is proposed on an hourly basis.

Hourly rate $55/hour
Typical migration 4–7 hours
Estimated total $220–$385

Estimated work

A straightforward migration is expected to fall within the range below. Individual steps may overlap, and actual time will depend on the current hosting environment and available access.

Task Estimated time
Review current hosting, domain/DNS configuration, and available access 0.5–1 hr
Create or verify a complete backup of the current website 0.5–1 hr
Set up the new hosting environment 0.5–1 hr
Transfer website files and configure the site on the new host 1–2 hrs
Test pages, images, links, and basic site functionality 0.5–1 hr
Configure SSL and complete the DNS cutover 0.5–1 hr
Post-migration verification and troubleshooting 0.5–1 hr
Typical straightforward migration 4–7 hrs
Cost control: If unexpected issues are discovered that are likely to push the work materially beyond the 7-hour estimate, I will discuss them with you before proceeding with the additional work.

What may require additional time

These items are not assumed to be necessary. If they are encountered, they would be billed at the same $55/hour rate after discussing the issue and likely additional time.

  • Repair or migration of legacy contact, newsletter, or other server-side scripts
  • Email migration or email/DNS reconfiguration
  • Troubleshooting undocumented server-specific functionality
  • Recovery of missing or non-public files from the existing host
  • Registrar, hosting-account, or access problems that require additional coordination

Access and working arrangement

The estimate assumes I can directly perform the technical steps once the necessary accounts are available. Primary passwords do not need to be shared where the provider supports temporary or delegated access.

If preferred, gallery staff can log into an account themselves and then allow me to work in the authenticated session. If the migration is performed with gallery staff operating the computer step-by-step, additional time may be required and is billed at the hourly rate.

Client-provided access

As needed for the migration, Visions Fine Art would provide access to:

  • The current hosting account
  • The new hosting account or environment
  • Domain and DNS management
  • Email administration only if email changes are required

Privacy and account information

Sensitive credentials, billing information, recovery codes, and similar account information will not be copied or shared. Account screenshots or other diagnostic information will not be shared with outside services without permission; when troubleshooting permits, account-specific details can be omitted or redacted.

Third-party costs

Hosting fees, domain-registration fees, email service, paid SSL certificates if required, and other third-party services are not included in the hourly rate and would be paid directly by Visions Fine Art. I can assist with selecting and configuring the appropriate services as part of the migration.

Scope: Proposal A is intended to migrate the existing website without redesigning it. Website redesign work is covered separately under Proposal B.
