Lisa Fitzgibbons

Lisa Fitzgibbons has been painting and teaching for twenty years, with a wide range of experience and style from landscape and still life to byzantine icons.

Traditional subjects as well as old world art are both of interest to her and she specializes in depth and texture. The unique old world frames that surround many of her pieces are also her own creation.

Lisa also teaches where beginning students get incredible results on their first try. She has a straight-forward approach and shares all her creative secrets and techniques.

She holds classes, workshops and private instruction throughout Arizona and her successful students number in the thousands.

Lisa attended Kent State University and continued her studies in Paris and Montreal. She has been featured on ABC's Sonoron Living TV and her work can be found in galleries throughout the nation, including murals for Neiman Marcus, The Franciscan Renewal Center and Best Western Hotels.
